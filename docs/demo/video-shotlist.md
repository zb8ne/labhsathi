## Framing rules used

- Browser chrome is always cropped out; every frame is a 16:9 crop of the page, scaled to 3840x2160.
- Wide shots keep the page's full width. The content (~x 60–1812) is wider than a 16:9 crop under the toolbar can hold, so those frames get thin top/bottom margins in the page's own background color instead of clipping the logo, headline, and Print button.
- Crops stay inside the page area measured in the recording: Chrome's toolbar border is rows 95–102, so every crop starts at row 104 or below, and x stays under 1880 to keep the scrollbar out.
- Scheme zooms are **holds on a settled still**, not live footage. A live crop drifted onto a different card mid-narration in v4, because the page kept moving under a fixed crop.
- Form typing is sped up (2–3x); clicks, results landing, and checklist ticks play at real speed.
- A scheme only gets a zoom if its on-screen reason actually matches Meena's profile. See the "deliberately left out" list in the script doc.
