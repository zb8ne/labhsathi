## Full narration, delivery order

1. Meena. Thirty-four. Unbanked, in rural Bihar.
2. She needs three departments. She gets to explain her life once.
3. Smart irrigation. Remote diagnostics. Micro-credit scoring. Three different builds — one shared blind spot: she has to already know it exists.
4. A twenty-thousand-rupee medicine bill can bankrupt a family that's never heard a five-lakh grant already covers it.
5. The technology was never the gap. The awareness was. LabhSathi closes it — one layer, under all three.
6. Agriculture. Health. Finance. One form. Three departments, answered.
7. It opens in Hindi first. English is one tap away.
8. Meena answers once. Her age, her income, her work, her state.
9. Then two things a document can't show: a kutcha house, and no bank account.
10. Two seconds later: twenty-eight schemes she may qualify for.
11. Each one says why it matched, and what to bring.
12. A pension, locked in while she's still under forty.
13. Ayushman Bharat: five lakh rupees, cashless. Not a guess. A reason.
14. For her daughter, a scholarship for every class she finishes.
15. A hundred days of guaranteed paid work, every year.
16. Help to turn her kutcha house into a pucca one.
17. Her first bank account, with accident insurance built in.
18. Twenty-eight in all, from departments she'd otherwise visit one at a time.
19. Not just a match. A checklist. Exactly what to bring, exactly where to go.
20. Every match links back to the real government page. Not our word. Theirs.
21. And one page to print, and carry to the counter.
22. The image is read once, then gone. Built to fail apart, on purpose.
23. No account. No password. Nothing to remember, nothing to lose.
24. If the scan ever fails, the form still works. You're never stuck waiting on one thing.
25. For a phone. A village kiosk. An NGO worker, reaching fifty people a day. LabhSathi — from document, to benefit.
