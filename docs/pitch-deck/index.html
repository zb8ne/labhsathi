01 The problem

India runs dozens of central welfare schemes.
Finding the ones you qualify for isn't the citizen's job to solve alone.

Scattered across ministries

PM-KISAN sits with Agriculture, PM-JAY with the National Health Authority, PMMVY with Women & Child Development — each on its own portal, its own eligibility PDF, its own document list.

Eligibility reads like legal text

Income slabs, category carve-outs, SECC 2011 criteria, state-specific top-ups — a household usually has to already know a scheme exists before it can check whether it qualifies.

The document, not the form, is the blocker

Income certificates, land records, caste certificates — people show up without the right paperwork, or don't apply because they're not sure what's required until they're already in line.

"Never store my documents"

Any tool that asks someone to upload an ID or income certificate is asking for trust it hasn't earned — most don't say what happens to that image after the read.

02 What LabhSathi does

Three steps, in either language.

  • 01

    Tell us your situation — or scan a document

    Income, occupation, family, category, land holding. Or scan an income certificate / ID and let extraction fill in what it can read — the form stays live underneath either way.

  • 02

    Matched against real scheme rules

    PM-KISAN, Ayushman Bharat, PMJDY, NSAP pensions, NSP scholarships, Sukanya Samriddhi, PMAY, PMMVY, PM-SYM — 11 schemes today, each with its own eligibility rule, not a keyword match.

  • 03

    A document checklist, and an honest badge

    Every match says "Worth checking," never "approved" — plus exactly why it matched, and what to bring. Verified links to the official government portal where we could actually confirm one exists.

The privacy guarantee is structural: the event schema has no Vec<u8> and no free-form JSON, so an image can't leak into a log line or a queue message — not because the code remembers to delete it, but because there's nowhere in the type for it to go.
